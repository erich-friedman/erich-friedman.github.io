Maximizing Minimum Light Intensity in a Circle

Consider the problem of placing n lights of unit brightness in a unit circle so that the minimum light intensity I any point in the circle receives (the sum of the squares of the reciprocals of the distances from the lights) is maximized. We show the best known configurations with the lights in red, intensity contours in black, and the points with minimum light intensity I in blue.
